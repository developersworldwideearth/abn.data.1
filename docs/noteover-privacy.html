Noteover — Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

The short version: Noteover keeps your notes on your own device. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

What the app stores

  • the notes you write, their colour and the section they belong to;
  • the sections you create and their order;
  • freehand marks you draw on a note;
  • comments captured during a recording take, with their timestamps;
  • your preferences — window position and size, paper opacity, text size, the pin shortcut, and whether the pen and auto-scroll are on.

All of it lives in a single SQLite database inside the app's own storage:

  • macOS: ~/Library/Containers/asia.abn.noteover/Data/Library/Application Support/asia.abn.noteover/onenote.db
  • iPhone and iPad: the app's private container, visible only to the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask for a name, email address or phone number.
  • It does not send your notes, or anything about them, anywhere. The app makes no network requests.
  • It contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs or third-party trackers.
  • It does not record your screen. Recording is done by whatever recorder you already use; Noteover only draws a window on top of it.

Permissions on macOS

The keyboard shortcut that pins the note works while another app is in front, which needs the Accessibility permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security. The app asks once, explains why, and works without it through the menu-bar icon. Noteover never requests Screen Recording, Microphone, Camera or Full Disk Access.

Exports you ask for

Backups (JSON) and shot lists (Markdown) are written to your Downloads folder only when you choose Export. What you do with those files afterwards is up to you.

Children

The app has no accounts and no user-generated content sharing, so it collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Your control over the data

  • Delete a note, a section or a take inside the app and it is removed from the database.
  • Export a JSON backup at any time from Preferences.
  • Deleting the app removes its database with it.

Changes

If a future version ever needs to change any of this — for example an optional sync feature — the policy will be updated here before that version ships, and the app will ask before sending anything off the device.
